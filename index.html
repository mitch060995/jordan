What we tile

Every surface.
Inside & out.

If it can be tiled, we can tile it. That covers wet areas where waterproofing matters most, pool shells that sit underwater all year, and sandstone paths that have to handle the weather. Each job is set out properly, laid dead level and finished with sharp, clean grout lines.

Bathrooms & ensuites

Full bathroom tiling from the screed up. That means compliant waterproofing, falls that actually drain, niches, and floor-to-ceiling walls with mitred corners.

  • Showers
  • Niches
  • Waterproofing
  • Mitred edges

Pools & spas

Waterline bands, full mosaic interiors, coping and pool surrounds. Everything is set with adhesives and grouts made to live underwater and handle chlorine and sun.

  • Waterline
  • Mosaics
  • Coping
  • Surrounds

Outdoor & crazy pave

Natural stone crazy paving, patios, alfresco areas, paths and steps. Each piece is cut and fitted by hand so the joints are even and the finish stays non-slip.

  • Crazy pave
  • Sandstone
  • Alfresco
  • Steps

Floors & large format

Living areas and hallways in big-format porcelain, laid flat with levelling systems so there are no lips, no hollow spots and no bumps underfoot.

  • 600×1200+
  • Levelling
  • Porcelain
  • Stone

Kitchens & splashbacks

Herringbone, chevron, stacked, subway or a single slab-look sheet. We cut clean around every powerpoint and window and finish the edges neatly.

  • Splashbacks
  • Herringbone
  • Laundries

Feature walls & repairs

Statement walls, fireplaces and entryways, plus regrouting, replacing cracked tiles, fixing leaking showers and bringing tired tiling back to life.

  • Feature walls
  • Regrouting
  • Leak repairs

How we work

From quote
to final polish

No surprises and no shortcuts. You'll know what's happening, when it's happening and what it costs before any work starts.

01

Free quote

Call, email or send photos through the form. We'll visit, measure up and give you a clear, itemised price with no obligation.

02

Prep & set out

Surfaces are levelled and waterproofed where needed. The layout is planned so cuts land in the right places and nothing looks like an afterthought.

03

Lay & grout

Tiles go down flat, square and fully bedded. Grout lines are consistent, silicone joints are neat and every edge is finished.

04

Clean & handover

We seal where needed, clean up properly and walk you through the finished job along with care tips to keep it looking new.

The standard on every job

Dead-level, every time

Laser set-out, levelling systems and proper bedding mean no lippage, no drummy tiles and no water sitting where it shouldn't.

Waterproofing done right

In wet areas the part you can't see matters most. Membranes, flashings and falls are done to standard before a single tile goes down.

Clean, considered cuts

The layout is planned around the room: centred features, balanced borders and mitred corners. No slivers tucked into the corners.
